Ember Escape

Tap to puff a glowing ember up a sooty chimney. How high can you climb?

A small ember is trapped at the bottom of an old chimney. Get it out.

Tap the screen to puff the ember upward and steer it through the soot. Tap the left side to blow it right, the right side to blow it left, or the middle to go straight up. The higher you climb, the tighter it gets.

  • Slip through gaps in soot bars, dodge spinning soot and sliding blocks, and wait for the iron dampers to open
  • Grab sparks for bonus points
  • Catch a white flame to burn straight through the next obstacle
  • The same chimney every run, so you can learn the route and beat your best. Or switch on Random layout for a new climb each time
  • A crackling fire soundtrack, and a setting to mute all game sounds
  • Plays offline. No accounts. Ads appear only on the menu screens, never while you climb

A one-tap arcade game for Android.
