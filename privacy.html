Ember Escape privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Ember Escape itself does not collect or store any personal information. The game saves your best scores and your settings (game sound, fire sound, invert taps, random layout) on your device only. They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Ember Escape shows banner ads on its menu screens, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), information about how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. See how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Permissions

The game uses your phone's vibration motor for tap feedback, and the internet connection to load ads. It does not access your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts or files.
